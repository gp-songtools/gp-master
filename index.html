Output format
Processing amount

Scales every automatic move: tone correction, compression, dynamic EQ, de-essing, saturation, clarity and auto width. Gentle also makes the dynamic EQ and de-esser react only to clear problems rather than running constantly.

Transients

Brings percussive hits forward relative to sustained sounds, or pushes them back. Measurable but subtle on already-compressed material — see the guide for what it can and cannot do.

Saturation

Auto keeps the gentle peak soft-clipping your amount setting controls. Light, Medium and Heavy add real harmonic saturation of the chosen character on top. Full master only.

Distortion

Blends a deliberately distorted copy under the whole mix. A creative effect, not a corrective one. Full master only. After separating stems you can distort individual stems instead, or as well.

Noise reduction

Removes steady hiss, hum and background noise. Mark a quiet passage with a Noise profile region above for the most reliable result, otherwise the noise floor is estimated from the whole song.

Processing mode

Full master analyzes the track, corrects its tone, tames boomy, muddy and harsh spots with dynamic EQ, balances lows, mids and highs with multiband compression, keeps the bass centered, adds light saturation, then true-peak limits to your target.
